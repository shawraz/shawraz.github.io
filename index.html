01Current research

Bieniasz Lab · Rockefeller University · 2024–present

A platform for nanobody
discovery & maturation.

I’m developing a synthetic, animal-free platform to discover and refine VHHs, also known as nanobodies. My work connects library design, in vitro selection, mammalian cell-based selection, and functional characterization.

01Design diversity
02Select binders
03Refine & validate
My contributions +
  • Designed and constructed diverse synthetic VHH libraries; enriched target-specific binders using ribosome display and affinity pulldown.
  • Engineered Ba/F3 and DHFR-knockout 293T selection systems that couple VHH–antigen recognition to cell survival.
  • Introduced targeted sequence variation using AID-T7-mediated mutagenesis and tracked enrichment with high-throughput sequencing.
  • Characterized antibody and nanobody function through protein-binding assays and SARS-CoV-2 pseudotyped virus neutralization.

02Previous research

Vuong Lab · City College of New York · 2022–2024

Understanding how B cells
shape antibody responses.

I investigated how the RNA helicase RHAU/DHX36 regulates AID-dependent class switch recombination and somatic hypermutation, using conditional knockout mice and ex vivo B-cell models.

My contributions +
  • Measured serum immunoglobulin responses following immunization and characterized class switching in purified B cells.
  • Quantified germline switch transcripts by RT-qPCR to study mechanisms underlying antibody diversification.
  • Immunophenotyped human PBMCs to characterize NK-cell exhaustion and B- and T-cell populations in chronic hepatitis B patients.
